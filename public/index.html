Set up once. Then let it run.

Your wallet signs every step that moves your funds. The agent and oracle sign only their own transactions.

01

Connect your wallet

Lace, Eternl or another CIP-30 wallet set to Preprod. Your keys stay in the wallet.

02

Fund your agent

Your own agent runs on this machine with its own key and pays its own transaction fees. The shared price oracle is paid for by the service, not by you.

03

Deploy the price oracle

Publishes the live ADA/USD price on-chain, cross-checked across Kraken, CoinGecko and Coinbase.

04

Open your loan

Lock ADA as collateral and borrow tUSD. Below health 1.0 anyone can liquidate it at the oracle price.

05

Sign the mandate and fund the vault

The vault holds rescue funds. These limits are written into its datum; the agent cannot change them.

Allowed recipients are fixed: your wallet and your own loan. The agent cannot pay anyone else.

06

Test the mandate, then start the agent

Prove the contract stops the agent before you trust it with your loan.
